EV & Mobility

Two websites, one obsession: understanding electric vehicles properly.

What started as sharing my passion for electric Audis grew into building one of the most detailed EV knowledge platforms on the web. Here is the story of both — plus my personal notes on living with EVs.

PROJECT · 2018 —

Electric Has Gone Audi

It started when I wanted to share my knowledge and passion about electric vehicles from Audi. The e-tron era was beginning, information was scattered, and I had opinions, data and daily driving experience nobody else was publishing.

Deep dives on every electric Audi — technology, charging, real-world range and the details the brochures skip.

Visit electrichasgoneaudi.net ↗ The story behind it
EVKX PROJECT · 2022 —

Knowledge eXchange for every EV

EVKX was created when I realized no site covered all brands in the detail I prefer. Specifications, charging curves, battery technology, comparisons and calculators — for every electric vehicle, not just one badge.

The KX stands for Knowledge eXchange: collect the knowledge properly once, then share it with everyone.
