About Rune

I am Rune T. Larsen, a Technology Architect and developer who has been building websites since 1996 — starting with a site dedicated to my mom's Mazda 323F. That was my first experience with HTML — cars and websites, apparently, from day one.

Technology and gadgets have been central interests throughout my life. Over the years, those interests have resulted in websites and projects covering home cinema, electric vehicles, software development, architecture and digital public services.

Some of these projects began as small ways to organize information for myself. Several became much larger than originally planned — hjemmekino.no taught Norwegians about AV technology in the early web years, and EVKX grew into a detailed knowledge platform for electric vehicles.

Since 2008 I have been building Altinn 2 and Altinn 3, Norway's digital public infrastructure. I told the full story in a detailed presentation for Altinn Kaffe — watch it on Vimeo — and back in 2020 I presented Altinn 3 together with two colleagues in this video. Today, I work with software architecture and development, contribute to large digital systems and maintain EVKX.

rtl.no is my personal home on the web. It is where I collect what I build, what I learn, the technology I find interesting, the music I return to and occasional stories from nearly three decades online.
