The main track uses 19 days: Ingolstadt gets two nights with a full Audi day on day five, without changing hotels or leaving luggage in the car. Day six starts very early and follows Timmelsjoch to one night in Bolzano, with Brenner as the fallback. Day seven combines Val Gardena and Passo Gardena with the stage to Verona/Lake Garda, and arrival home from Kolding/Vejle is on day nineteen. Zurich remains the fallback if the Swiss pass day should not finish in Vaduz.
