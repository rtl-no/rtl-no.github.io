The main track uses about 21 days: six days through Munich, Innsbruck and Sölden on day seven, four days through Timmelsjoch, the Dolomites and Verona, four days with Antibes as the Riviera base, three days through Andermatt, the passes, Vaduz and Frankfurt, and four days through Amsterdam, Kolding/Vejle and home. The most natural compression is removing the Riviera buffer day; Zurich remains the fallback if the pass day should not finish in Vaduz.
