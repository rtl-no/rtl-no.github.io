The main track uses about 19 days: six days through Munich, five through the Alps, northern Italy and the Riviera, three to return to and through Switzerland, and five via Frankfurt, Amsterdam and home. An extra Riviera day and a gentler stop at Lake Garda can make it a 21–22-day trip; the modules can be shortened without breaking the whole journey.
