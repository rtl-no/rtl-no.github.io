Electric Has Gone Audi is an active project for sharing knowledge and enthusiasm about Audi and the brand's transition to electric mobility.

I am now on my third electric Audi. That makes the project more than a collection of news and specifications: its perspective also comes from ownership, charging, software, long-distance travel and everyday use over time.

The project combines ownership experience, vehicle technology, specifications and the small practical details that manufacturers often explain poorly. The work was also an important part of the path toward the broader, brand-independent EVKX, but EVKX did not replace Electric Has Gone Audi. The projects have different perspectives and continue side by side.
