Electric Has Gone Audi began as a place to share knowledge and enthusiasm about Audi's move into electric vehicles.

It combined ownership experience, vehicle technology, specifications and the small practical details that manufacturers often explain poorly. The work eventually led toward the broader, brand-independent scope of EVKX.
