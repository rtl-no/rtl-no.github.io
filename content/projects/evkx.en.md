EVKX began because no existing site covered every electric-vehicle brand in the level of detail I wanted. The name means **EV Knowledge eXchange**.

The project brings specifications, charging data, comparisons, calculators and technical explanations into one structured platform. It follows a familiar pattern: understand a subject properly, organize the knowledge, and build something useful around it.

## What it became

What started as a personal knowledge project grew into a large global reference. The work spans software architecture, data modelling, editorial workflows, translation, search and the practical use of AI.
