I started working with Altinn 2 in 2008. In 2015 I joined the work on Altinn 3, from its early stages and onwards through the development of the new platform.

## From Altinn 2 to Altinn 3

My work with Altinn has given me experience with the long-term development of critical public digital services. The transition from Altinn 2 to Altinn 3 has involved modernization, new architectural choices and the development of a platform designed to work across the public sector.

I have been actively involved in a wide range of areas over the years. In recent years, my main focus has been authentication and authorization: how users and systems establish their identity, and how they receive the appropriate access to data and services.

## Experience and reflections

This part of rtl.no is for personal technical reflections on architecture, authentication, authorization, modernization, platform development and what long-running systems can teach us.
