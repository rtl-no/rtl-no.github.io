I started hjemmekino.no in 1997. My interest in picture, sound and film had grown to the point where I wanted to collect what I learned and explain the technology on the web.

It began as a personal project at a time when both the web and home cinema as a field of interest were developing rapidly. The site became a place for guides, explanations and advice on creating a better cinema experience at home.

## Part of Akers Mic from 1998 to 2004

From 1998 to 2004, hjemmekino.no was part of Akers Mic, where I worked as web editor. The connection gave the site a clear place in a professional audio and video environment while I continued building its content and knowledge base.

Since 2004, hjemmekino.no has been independent. That format made it possible to continue with the same basic idea: understand the technology, organize the knowledge and make it available to others.

## Two appearances on NRK

In 2007, I appeared twice on NRK's consumer programme *Forbrukerinspektørene*, one week apart:

- [26 September 2007: HDTV](https://tv.nrk.no/serie/forbrukerinspektoerene/sesong/2007/episode/OFAA32003907) – I explained HDTV and what the new television technology meant for consumers.
- [3 October 2007: Blu-ray and HD DVD](https://tv.nrk.no/serie/forbrukerinspektoerene/sesong/2007/episode/OFAA32004007) – I discussed the competing formats for high-definition films.

Both topics were new and surrounded by terminology and format choices that were difficult to navigate. This was exactly the kind of technology hjemmekino.no was created to explain.

## A long-term knowledge project

hjemmekino.no established a pattern I later carried into other projects: study the details, structure the information and publish the result on an independent website.

Today, [hjemmekino.no](https://hjemmekino.no) remains an independent resource with guides covering audio, video, rooms, acoustics, setup and calibration.
